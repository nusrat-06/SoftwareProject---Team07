## Problem
University students in the United Arab Emirates (UAE) face significant information fragmentation when searching for internships and entry-level jobs. Currently, postings are scattered across external portals (such as Bayt, LinkedIn, and Naukrigulf) and separate university email announcements. Students at Abu Dhabi University (ADU) lose time navigating multiple platforms, struggle to track application deadlines manually, and often submit resumes that are not tailored to UAE recruiter expectations. **ADU CareerLink** centralizes local opportunities and streamlines the application process into one platform built specifically for ADU students.

---

## Key Features
* **Smart Job Aggregator:** Consolidates UAE-based internships and entry-level positions into a central feed with filtering by campus location (Abu Dhabi, Al Ain, Dubai), job type, and Emiratization alignment.
* **Application Tracker:** Provides a centralized dashboard where students can log applications, attach resume notes/documents, and update statuses (*Applied*, *Interviewing*, *Offered*, *Rejected*).
* **Skills Matcher & Resume Builder:** Compares a student's profile skills against job requirements to highlight skill gaps and provides UAE-optimized resume templates.
* **Company Insights & Alumni Hub:** Displays local company profiles, crowdsourced student interview feedback, and an ADU alumni connection directory.
